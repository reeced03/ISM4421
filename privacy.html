Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. The weather, map and favorites all work without it. If you sign in, Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with this site, and it uses them only to show who is signed in.

That information is saved in your own browser's local storage. It isn't sent to a Trailhead Weather server or database, and it isn't sold or shared. Choosing Sign out removes it from your browser.

To stop this site's access to your Google account, go to your Google account connections. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Preferences

Your favorite parks, temperature units, view and theme are saved in your browser, and weather responses are cached there for 15 minutes. You can clear them at any time through your browser's settings.

Other services

Your browser connects directly to these services, and each one can see your IP address:

  • Netlify hosts the site.
  • Open-Meteo provides weather and air quality data.
  • CARTO provides map tiles.
  • Google provides fonts and the sign-in button.
  • cdnjs delivers the map library.
